## 3. Detailed Tasks & Specifications

### Task 1.1: Soft Clay Tokens (`kayo.clay`)
Add the soft clay dual-shadow primitives to `tokens/src/kaayo.json`.
In Kaayo, soft clay is light source top-left with opaque brand tints:
- **Raised SM**: `boxShadow: -2px -2px 4px 0px {color.neutral.25}, 2px 3px 6px 0px {color.secondary.200}`
- **Raised MD**: `boxShadow: -4px -4px 10px 0px {color.neutral.25}, 5px 6px 14px 0px {color.secondary.200}`
- **Raised LG**: `boxShadow: -6px -6px 14px 0px {color.neutral.25}, 8px 10px 22px 0px {color.secondary.200}`
- **Inset SM**: `boxShadow: inset 2px 2px 4px 0px {color.secondary.200}, inset -2px -2px 4px 0px {color.neutral.25}`
- **Inset MD**: `boxShadow: inset 3px 3px 6px 0px {color.secondary.200}, inset -3px -3px 6px 0px {color.neutral.25}`
- **Inset LG**: `boxShadow: inset 4px 4px 10px 0px {color.secondary.200}, inset -4px -4px 8px 0px {color.neutral.25}`
- **Outlined Card**: `boxShadow: 2px 2px 0px 0px {color.secondary.700}, -2px -2px 0px 0px {color.secondary.200}, 8px 10px 22px 0px {color.secondary.200}`

### Task 1.2: Brutalist Shadow Extensions
